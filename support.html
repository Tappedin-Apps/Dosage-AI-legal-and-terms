Frequently Asked Questions

How do I delete my account?

Open Dosed AI on your device, tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation, then tap Delete Account. You'll be asked to type "DELETE" to confirm. Once confirmed, your account and all associated data (profile, survey answers, plans, injection logs) are permanently removed within 30 days. This action cannot be undone.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. To cancel:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone
  2. Tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions
  3. Find Dosed AI in the list
  4. Tap Cancel Subscription

You'll keep access to paid features until the end of your current billing period.

How do I request a refund?

All refunds are processed by Apple. To request one:

  1. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com
  2. Sign in with your Apple ID
  3. Find your Dosed AI purchase and select "Request a Refund"

Apple decides whether to grant the refund based on their refund policy.

I'm having trouble signing in

Make sure you're using the same email address you signed up with. If you've forgotten your password, contact us at doseaiapp@gmail.com and we'll help you recover access. Password reset within the app is coming in a future update.

My injection log isn't syncing

Logs sync automatically when you have an internet connection. If you logged an injection while offline, it will sync the next time the app reaches the network. If logs still don't appear after reconnecting, try fully closing and reopening the app. If the issue persists, contact us.

The app shows "You're offline"

Check that your device has an active internet connection (Wi-Fi or cellular). The app needs to reach our servers to sign you in and load your data. Tap "Retry" once you're back online. The Dashboard screen continues to show your most recent cached data even when offline.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Email doseaiapp@gmail.com with the subject "Data Export Request" and we'll provide your injection logs and health profile in CSV format within 30 days.

Is Dosed AI medical advice?

No. Dosed AI is an informational tracking tool. It is not a medical device, does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and is not a substitute for consultation with a licensed healthcare provider. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before starting, changing, or stopping any peptide regimen.

Is my health data secure?

Yes. All data is encrypted in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and at rest in our database. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers. See our Privacy Policy for full details on what we collect and how we protect it.

Who is Dosed AI for?

Dosed AI is intended for adults aged 18 and older who are tracking their own peptide protocols. It is not intended for use by minors, pregnant or breastfeeding individuals (without explicit healthcare provider guidance), or anyone using the app as a substitute for medical care.
