Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 3, 2026  ·  tappedin-apps.github.io/Dosage-AI-legal-and-terms/privacy.html

Health Data Notice. Dosed AI handles sensitive health information you provide voluntarily. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why, and how it is stored and shared. Please read it carefully.

1. Who We Are

Dosed AI ("we," "us," or "our") is operated by Samuel Chu, sole proprietor, located at 7201 Rampart Lane, La Palma, CA 90623, United States. We operate the Dosed AI mobile application. Our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service are published at tappedin-apps.github.io/Dosage-AI-legal-and-terms/. If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at doseaiapp@gmail.com.

2. Who This Policy Applies To

This policy applies to all users of the Dosed AI application. Dosed AI is intended for adults aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, contact us immediately and we will delete it.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Account Information

  • Email address (used for authentication and account management)
  • Server-assigned user identifier (used internally to associate your data with your account)

3.2 Health Profile

Information you enter when creating your profile:

  • Age
  • Biological sex
  • Body weight
  • Activity level

3.3 Survey & Medical Background

Information collected through in-app intake surveys:

  • Medical conditions (self-reported)
  • Current medications
  • Known allergies
  • Pregnancy or breastfeeding status
  • Health goals

Health data disclaimer: All health information in Dosed AI is self-reported by you. We do not verify, validate, or independently obtain medical records. We cannot confirm the accuracy of any information you provide.

3.4 AI-Generated Plans

  • Peptide plans generated by the AI based on your profile and survey answers

3.5 Injection Logs

Data you log manually within the app:

  • Peptide name and dose
  • Injection site
  • Date and time
  • Personal notes

3.6 Subscription & Purchase Data

  • Subscription status and entitlements (managed by RevenueCat — see Section 5)
  • We do not store raw payment card information

3.7 Technical Data

  • Device type and operating system version
  • App version
  • Crash reports and error logs

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the service: Account creation, authentication, and personalized AI plan generation
  • To generate AI plans: Your survey answers and health profile are sent to our AI providers (see Section 5) to produce peptide recommendations
  • To manage subscriptions: Verifying entitlements and processing renewals
  • To communicate with you: Transactional emails (account confirmation, support responses)
  • To comply with law: Responding to lawful requests from regulatory or law enforcement authorities

We do not sell your personal data. Dosed AI does not train its own AI models on your data. With respect to our third-party AI providers (see Section 5.3), OpenAI does not use API inputs to train its models under their standard API terms. Google Gemini's data handling is governed by the API terms applicable to our key tier; we do not opt in to data sharing for training where that option exists. Dosed AI does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we do not use the Apple Advertising Identifier (IDFA).

5. Third-Party Services

5.1 Supabase (Database & Authentication)

All user data — including your email, health profile, survey answers, AI-generated plans, and injection logs — is stored in a Supabase-hosted PostgreSQL database (cloud-hosted). Supabase also handles authentication. Data is stored in encrypted-at-rest databases. Supabase's privacy policy is available at supabase.com/privacy.

5.2 RevenueCat (Subscription & Payments)

Subscription management and in-app purchase processing is handled by RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives your device identifier and subscription status to verify entitlements. We do not share your health data with RevenueCat. RevenueCat's privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

5.3 OpenAI and Google (AI Plan Generation)

To generate personalized peptide plans, your survey answers and health profile are transmitted to third-party AI services. Our primary provider is OpenAI (GPT-4o-mini model); Google Gemini (1.5 Pro model) is used as a fallback when OpenAI is unavailable. This data is sent over encrypted connections and used solely to generate your plan. OpenAI does not use API inputs to train its models under their standard API terms. Google Gemini's data handling is governed by the API terms applicable to our key tier; we do not opt in to data sharing for training where that option exists. See:

  • OpenAI Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

5.4 Sentry (Error Monitoring)

We use Sentry for crash reporting, error monitoring, and performance tracing. Sentry receives crash data, error logs, performance metrics, and device/browser metadata when errors occur. Session replay data may be captured during error sessions to help us diagnose issues; all text content in replays is masked to protect your privacy. Sentry does not receive your health data, injection logs, or survey answers. Sentry's privacy policy is available at sentry.io/privacy.

5.5 On-Device Storage

Certain data — including your AI-generated plan, survey answers, theme preference, and notification settings — may be cached locally on your device for offline access and performance. This data is stored in your browser's local storage or the device's native preferences and is not transmitted to any third party. You can clear this data by deleting your account or reinstalling the app.

5.6 Local Notifications

If you enable injection reminders, Dosed AI uses your device's local notification system (Apple's iOS notification service) to display scheduled reminders. Notification content is generic ("Time for your scheduled dose") and does not include peptide names, doses, or other health information on the lock screen. Reminders are scheduled and delivered entirely on-device — the contents of your notifications are not transmitted to our servers or to any third party. You can disable reminders at any time through the app's Settings or your device's system Settings.

Data protection commitment: Each of our third-party service providers is contractually required to provide the same or equivalent level of protection for your personal data as described in this Privacy Policy.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account:

  • Your personal data (profile, survey answers, injection logs, AI plans) will be deleted within 30 days of the deletion request
  • Anonymized or aggregated data that cannot identify you may be retained indefinitely
  • We may retain certain data where required by law or for legitimate fraud prevention purposes

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

7.1 Access

You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you by contacting doseaiapp@gmail.com.

7.2 Export

You can request an export of your injection logs and health profile in CSV format. We will respond to export requests within 30 days.

7.3 Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and all associated personal data. Submit a deletion request through the app settings or by emailing doseaiapp@gmail.com.

7.4 Correction

You can update or correct your health profile and account information at any time within the app.

7.5 Portability

Where technically feasible, we will provide your data in a structured, machine-readable format upon request.

  • International transfers: Your data may be transferred to and processed in the United States and other countries where our service providers operate. We rely on standard contractual clauses or equivalent mechanisms for such transfers
  • Right to lodge a complaint: You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise your GDPR rights, contact us at doseaiapp@gmail.com.

9. CCPA — California Residents

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) grants you the following rights:

  • Right to Know: The right to know what personal information we collect, use, disclose, and sell
  • Right to Delete: The right to request deletion of your personal information
  • Right to Opt-Out: We do not sell personal information. You do not need to opt out
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights

To exercise these rights, contact doseaiapp@gmail.com. We will respond within 45 days of a verifiable request.

10. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encryption of data in transit (TLS/HTTPS)
  • Encryption of data at rest in our database
  • Role-based access controls limiting who can access user data

No system is completely secure. In the event of a data breach that affects your personal data, we will notify affected users as required by applicable law.
